# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/.../2z5v-ecg8  (pulled 2026-10-06)
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain (Data Provided by City of Seattle)

## Questions I asked
- When is the Burke-Gilman trail the busiest, and when is it the quietest?
- Do cyclists and pedestrians use the Burke-Gilman trail at different times of the day?
- Is there evidence people use the Burke-Gilman trail to commute, not just for fun/recreation?
- What are the directional traffic patterns throughout the day for cyclists and pedestrians? What can be inferred from those patterns?

## How to re-run
Open `A1.ipynb` and run all cells (needs `requests`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`).
